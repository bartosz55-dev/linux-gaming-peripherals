# SteelSeries Aerox 3 Hardware Architecture

## Sensor
- **Model**: SteelSeries TrueMove Air (custom PixArt PAW3335)
- **CPI Range**: 100 - 18,000 in 100 CPI Increments
- **IPS**: 400 on SteelSeries QcK surfaces
- **Acceleration**: 40G
- **Polling Rate**: 1000Hz (1ms), 500Hz (2ms), 250Hz (4ms), 125Hz (8ms)
- **Hardware Acceleration**: None (Zero Hardware Acceleration)

## Illumination
- 3 independent PrismSync RGB zones with diffusion bar under the honeycomb mesh:
  - **Zone 1 (Top / Z1)**: Scroll wheel and forward cavity
  - **Zone 2 (Middle / Z2)**: Central honeycomb chamber
  - **Zone 3 (Bottom / Z3)**: Rear diffuser and underglow perimeter

## Wireless Dual Connectivity
- **2.4 GHz Quantum 2.0 Wireless**: Ultra-low latency 1000Hz connection via USB-C dongle (`1038:1838`).
- **Bluetooth 5.0**: Standard Bluetooth LE mode.
- **Wired USB**: USB-C Super Mesh Cable (`1038:183A`).

## Battery
- Up to 200 hours in Bluetooth mode, 80 hours in 2.4 GHz mode.
- Fast charging via USB-C (15 minutes gives ~40 hours of gameplay).
